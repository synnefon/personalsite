oura heartrate tool — privacy policy

this is a personal-use tool on connor hopkins' personal website. when you connect your oura account, your browser fetches your data directly from the oura api and renders it on the page. that's the whole pipeline.

  • no data ever leaves your browser — there is no server, database, or backend
  • nothing is logged, tracked, analyzed, shared, or sold
  • your oura access token is kept in your browser's localStorage so you stay connected between visits; hitting "disconnect" deletes it
  • downloads (csv/json) are generated locally and saved to your own machine
